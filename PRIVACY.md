# Gloop Pop privacy policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

Gloop Pop is a puzzle game for the web and Android (`net.aieat.gloop_pop`). It does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

- No accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.
- The game makes no network requests for gameplay. Your scores, settings and progress are saved only on your device.
- On Android, the system's Auto Backup may copy your game save to your own Google account backup. The developer cannot see it.
- When you choose to share a score, the game hands an image or text to your device's share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

To remove all game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it (Android), or clear site data for this site (web).

Questions: open an issue at [github.com/AieatAssam/gloop-pop/issues](https://github.com/AieatAssam/gloop-pop/issues).

Published copy (the URL used in Google Play): https://aieatassam.github.io/gloop-pop/privacy.html (source: `public/privacy.html`; keep both in sync).
